宿舍改造家：把约束走成一间寝室

## 选题简介

面向中国高校学生的寝室改造网页。说清床位、房型、预算、轮椅进出和北方冬季采光（例如吉林的窗朝向），或放一张参考照片，就能得到一间可以走动、更换家具、并导出室友讨论图的三维寝室。核心家具由 Aholo Lux3D 按文生三维生成；没有密钥时使用标明「演示替身」的本地模型，不会把官方文档示例当成真实生成结果。

## English gloss (do not paste over the Chinese fields unless the form is the global site)

**Title:** Dorm Remix: walk the constraints into a room

**Summary:** A web app for Chinese university students. Beds, room type, budget, wheelchair access, and winter light (for example a window in Jilin), or a reference photo, become a 3D dorm you can walk, refurnish, and export for a roommate meeting. Hero furniture is generated with Aholo Lux3D text-to-3D. Without a key, the app uses stand-ins that are labeled as such, and official documentation samples are never presented as this project’s results.
